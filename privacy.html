Astridr Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-15

Who this covers

Astridr is a personal, self-hosted assistant used only by its owner, Larry Mandras. It has no other users.

Google data it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google OAuth, Astridr requests these YouTube scopes:

  • youtube.readonly: read the owner's channel and video information;
  • youtube.upload: upload videos the owner has approved;
  • youtube.force-ssl: manage the owner's own videos (for example titles, descriptions and comments).

How the data is used and stored

The data is used only to perform the YouTube tasks the owner asks for. OAuth tokens and any YouTube data are stored only on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or model training.

Retention and removal

Tokens stay on the owner's machine until he removes them. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, after which the stored tokens stop working.

Astridr's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
